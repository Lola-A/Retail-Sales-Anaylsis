## 📘 Project Overview  
This project explores a fictional retail company’s sales dataset to uncover insights into revenue trends, regional performance, and product profitability.  
The goal is to demonstrate business analysis and data analytics skills by transforming raw data into actionable insights that inform business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
1. Identify the most profitable product lines and regions.  
2. Analyze monthly sales performance over time.  
3. Visualize key performance indicators (KPIs) in interactive dashboards.  
4. Communicate data-driven recommendations clearly and professionally.

---

## 🧠 Business Questions  
- Which regions generate the highest total sales revenue?  
- Which product lines are most profitable overall?  
- How do monthly and yearly sales trends fluctuate across time?  
- What strategic insights can improve inventory or marketing decisions?

## 🔍 Process Workflow  
1. **Data Sourcing** – Used the *Sales_Data_Sample.csv* dataset from Kaggle.  
2. **SQL Analysis** – Aggregated data by region, product line, and month using BigQuery.  
3. **Data Cleaning in R** – Removed missing values, standardized columns, and exported a cleaned dataset for visualization.  
4. **Excel Pivot Tables** – Built dashboards summarizing revenue by product line, country, and monthly trends.  
5. **Tableau Visualization** – Created an interactive dashboard highlighting key KPIs for executive-level insights.  
6. **Insights & Recommendations** – Interpreted results and presented findings in a concise business context.

---

## 📈 Key Insights  
- **Top Regions:** North America and Europe contribute the majority of total revenue.  
- **Best-Selling Product Line:** Classic Cars generated the highest consistent sales across multiple years.  
- **Seasonal Trend:** Sales peak during Q4 each year, suggesting increased holiday demand.  
- **Revenue Opportunity:** Realigning marketing and inventory focus toward high-performing regions could increase annual revenue by **12–15%**.
